01 · Introduction

What is the
Innovation Lab?

The Innovation Lab is a multi-disciplinary software and technology development environment. It is not a collection of separate mini-projects — it is a single, long-term integrated platform built across multiple technology domains simultaneously.

Every team, every intern, every lead contributes to one unified product — the AgriChain Smart System, the Innovation Lab flagship project. The platform connects AI, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, IoT (including drone monitoring), software engineering, and optional blockchain traceability into a coherent, working system over 6 months (24 weeks).

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Internet of Things (IoT) Drone Technology (IoT sub-stream) Cloud Computing Software Engineering Cybersecurity Blockchain & Traceability

03 · Work Model

How Work
Flows

1

Operations Team Sets Direction

The Operations Team defines the long-term milestone — what the system must achieve and by when.

Example: "Build an AI-powered smart platform with a secure cloud backend and real-time IoT data feeds."
2

Teams Clarify Roles & Research Options

At kickoff, each team clears role ownership, then researches technology and simulation options for its domain before implementation begins.

Rule: Technology stacks are recommended guidance, not compulsory. Alternatives are allowed with justification.
3

Feasibility Review & Operations Approval

Each team runs an internal feasibility discussion, then presents findings to Operations for approval on architecture, tooling, and integration impacts.

Approval Gate: Team Members -> Team Leads -> Operations Lead -> Project Manager
4

Teams Execute, Collaborate & Submit

After approval, teams execute tasks in Planner and collaborate across domains for shared APIs, data contracts, security controls, and deployment readiness.

Output: Code commits, design files, test reports, documentation — all tracked in Planner.

06 · Flexibility Policy

Adjusted
Expectations

Who Qualifies?

Some interns are in-service training, enrolled as full-time students, or have limited availability due to external commitments.

These members remain active contributors, but with adjusted expectations tailored to their situation.

What Changes?

  • Tasks are scoped to fit available hours
  • Deadlines are extended where appropriate
  • Work is completed asynchronously
  • Contribution is still tracked and valued
  • Communication with Team Lead is expected regularly
07 · Task Management

Microsoft
Planner

All work MUST be tracked in Microsoft Planner — no exceptions.

Every task that exists must exist in Planner. No verbal assignments. No unofficial to-do lists. If it isn't in Planner, it doesn't officially exist.
